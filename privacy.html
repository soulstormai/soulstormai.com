Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

This policy covers soulstormai.com, the site operated as SoulstormAI ("we," "our," or "us"). It describes what this website collects, how Google advertising and cookies work here, and how to reach us.

TipSplit (tipsplit.soulstormai.com), the SideGig Calculator (sidehustlecalc.soulstormai.com), FamiliarBank (familiarbank.com), and WebsiteMob (websitemob.com) are separate sites. Their practices are their own. This policy does not speak for them.

1. Information We Collect

This website does not have user accounts, and it does not ask for a password or a credit card.

Information you choose to send:

  • Email: If you write to info@soulstormai.com, we receive whatever you put in that message, including your email address.
  • Newsletter: The homepage signup asks for a name and an email address and submits them to a Google Form. We use that list to send notes about the site. There is no separate marketing platform on this domain.

Information that stays on your device:

  • Calculator inputs: Numbers you type into the house-flip calculator, the loan calculator, and similar tools on this domain are processed in your browser. Saved deals use the browser’s local storage. They are not uploaded to a SoulstormAI server.
  • Theme and newsletter prompt: The site stores a theme choice and whether you have dismissed the newsletter prompt in local storage.

Information collected automatically:

  • Server logs: The host (Vercel) receives standard request data such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, as part of operating the site.
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-RF30DV7CY0) to see which pages are visited. Google’s policy is linked below. The interactive storm at /storm/ does not load that tag. The October 22 version of the page left it out so audio playback would not trip an extra Safari privacy prompt.
  • Cookies used for ads: Google AdSense, if ads are being served, sets and reads cookies. The next sections describe that in detail.

Affiliate links:

  • Some outbound links are affiliate links. The merchant may learn that the click came from this site. We do not receive your account details at that merchant.

5. Cookies, including Google ads

A cookie is a small file a site or a service provider stores on your browser. This site uses cookies in three ways:

  • On-device preferences. Local storage (a related browser feature, not always a cookie) remembers the light or dark theme and whether the newsletter prompt has been closed. That data stays in your browser.
  • Analytics cookies. Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits and to report aggregate page use. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Advertising cookies from Google. Most pages include the Google AdSense script for publisher ca-pub-3424397100313583. The storm page does not. When ads run, Google and its partners may use cookies — including third-party cookies — to serve ads, to cap how often you see an ad, and to measure whether an ad was viewed or clicked. Those cookies can remember that you visited this site. Ads may be based on your visits to this site and to other sites.

You can control personalized ads at Google Ads Settings. You can also refuse or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking cookies can stop ads from measuring or personalizing; the articles and calculators on this site still load.

Google’s description of advertising cookies is here: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may see a consent message before Google uses cookies for ads. That message is configured in the publisher’s Google AdSense account. If you do not see one and you believe you should, email us.

6. Data Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Calculator entries that stay in your browser are only as private as that browser and device. Email you send us is as private as email is. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

7. Data Retention

You can ask us to delete a newsletter signup or an email thread. Analytics and ad data are kept by Google under Google’s retention settings, which we do not fully control.
