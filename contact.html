Questions that have a direct answer

Is there an account to cancel?

Not on soulstormai.com. The calculators do not ask you to sign up. Saved deals stay in your browser’s local storage. Clearing site data removes them. TipSplit, SideGig Calculator, and FamiliarBank are separate sites; look on those sites for their own account rules.

Can I get a refund?

There is nothing to buy on this domain. If a different site charged you, contact that site. Affiliate purchases are between you and the merchant.

Where do I opt out of ads?

Ad choices are described in the privacy policy, including Google’s ads settings. This website does not have a separate login where ad personalization can be switched off.

Partnerships and press

Email info@soulstormai.com with “Partnership” or “Press” in the subject and say what you want. A reply is not guaranteed.

Privacy requests

To ask what this site holds about you, or to ask for a newsletter signup to be deleted, email info@soulstormai.com from the address you used. The privacy policy explains what the site actually collects. Calculator inputs are not sent to us.
