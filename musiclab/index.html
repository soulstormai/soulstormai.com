Teaching Claude AI to sing

A public lab log. Each entry records one new skill Claude AI learned, followed by the exact song it produced afterward. Nothing is edited by hand, and the failures stay in.

I'm running this study with Claude AI by Anthropic. Claude writes the code, composes the songs and logs what it learns. I listen and give the verdicts.

Method. Every song is composed as MIDI, played on open-license soundfonts (GeneralUser GS), and rendered in software. Vocals come from an open-source speaking voice that Claude AI retunes note by note. One human reviewer listens to each result and gives a verdict.
LOG 0118:40 CSTComposition

First composition from a blank file

Claude AI learned
  • Write a real MIDI file: chords, bass line, melody and drums on separate channels
  • Arrange a song in sections that build and fall: intro, verse, chorus, outro
  • Turn energy into arrangement: calm sections hold long chords, full sections add a bass groove and a crash cymbal

LOG 0521:14 CSTTechnique

Vocal technique, part one

Claude AI learned
  • Hold the vowels and keep the consonants quick: “li-i-ights”, not “llllights”
  • Slide into each note from the one before it
  • Add vibrato only on long notes, the way trained singers do
  • Mix like a studio: double-tracked vocal, reverb, EQ and compression

LOG 0621:18 CSTTechnique

Sing the way you speak

Claude AI learned
  • Sing a whole line in one breath, so words flow into each other
  • Find each spoken syllable and give it its own note
  • Keep a quarter of the speaking voice's own expression
  • Write in call and answer: one line ends up in the air, the next resolves home
  • Breathe before each line

LOG 0721:50 CSTFull song

A complete song from a lyric sheet

Claude AI learned
  • Blend genres: a dance beat with British pop-rock chords, including the classic I → ♭VII → IV move
  • Sing a full lyric sheet of 36 lines, placed section by section: verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, final chorus
  • Shape the melody by section: low verses, higher choruses, a half-energy bridge
  • Fit eight syllables to a bar on fast dance tempos
