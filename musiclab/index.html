SoulstormAI · Research study 001

Teaching an AI to sing

A public lab log. Each entry records one new skill the AI learned, followed by the exact song it produced afterward. Nothing is edited by hand, and the failures stay in.

Started Oct 5, 2026 Experiments 6 Cost per song $0.00 Human singers 0
Method. Every song is composed as MIDI, played on open-license soundfonts (GeneralUser GS), and rendered in software. Vocals come from an open-source speaking voice that the AI retunes note by note. One human reviewer listens to each result and gives a verdict.
LOG 0118:40 CSTComposition

First composition from a blank file

AI learned
  • Write a real MIDI file: chords, bass line, melody and drums on separate channels
  • Arrange a song in sections that build and fall: intro, verse, chorus, outro
  • Turn energy into arrangement: calm sections hold long chords, full sections add a bass groove and a crash cymbal
“San Antonio Midnight” · instrumentalEb major · 84 BPM · 1:08
VerdictA usable lo-fi track on the first try.
LOG 0220:46 CSTVoices

Wordless vocals

AI learned
  • Add a choir “aah” that doubles the melody in the chorus
  • Hold backing “oohs” under the verse chords and the outro

LOG 0421:05 CSTPrompts

Songs from a written scene

AI learned
  • Read a video-style prompt: scene, character, sings: “lyrics in quotes”, plus an Audio line
  • Choose a male or female voice from the character description
  • Use a mood grid where energy sets the tempo and mood sets major or minor, then pick a genre from that
“I Been On My Own” · Vibe: energy 7, mood 2trap · minor · 118 BPM · 0:42
VerdictThe mood is right. The singer is lost.
LOG 0521:14 CSTTechnique

Vocal technique, part one

AI learned
  • Hold the vowels and keep the consonants quick: “li-i-ights”, not “llllights”
  • Slide into each note from the one before it
  • Add vibrato only on long notes, the way trained singers do
  • Mix like a studio: double-tracked vocal, reverb, EQ and compression
“Midnight in the City” · v2lo-fi · 76 BPM · 0:51
Verdict“Getting better, but you talk more naturally than it sings.”
LOG 0621:18 CSTTechnique

Sing the way you speak

AI learned
  • Sing a whole line in one breath, so words flow into each other
  • Find each spoken syllable and give it its own note
  • Keep a quarter of the speaking voice's own expression
  • Write in call and answer: one line ends up in the air, the next resolves home
  • Breathe before each line
“Midnight in the City” · v3lo-fi · 76 BPM · 0:43
Verdict“Hahaha. Hilarious, out of tune and out of rhythm.”
