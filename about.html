About SoulstormAI

SoulstormAI publishes small tools and the notes that explain them. The useful pages are calculators, a music lab, one generative art piece, and essays. The calculators on this domain run in your browser.

What is actually on the site

House Flip Calculator

A worksheet for a single flip: purchase, rehab, holding, selling, and financing. It checks the 70% rule against the numbers you type and can save deals in this browser. State presets are round examples so the form is not empty. They are not a market report. Replace them with the property in front of you. Open it.

Loan Calculator

Fix-and-flip, rental, construction, and bridge sketches. Interest, points, and rent are inputs, not looked-up rates. Open it.

TipSplit

Bill splitting when the plates and the drinks are not the same. The method is written out on this site. The calculator itself is at tipsplit.soulstormai.com.

Side gig math

How to turn a payout into an hourly rate after costs, unpaid time, and a tax set-aside you choose. The worksheet links to the SideGig Calculator.

FamiliarBank

A household budget method — income, fixed bills, variable spending, savings rate — and a link to the calculator on familiarbank.com. Read the method.

Pet travel notes

A checklist for hotel policies: fees, weight limits, and what to ask before you pay. An older PetWander host is not linked, because its certificate is expired. Use the checklist.

Music Lab and Borrowed Voice

Music Lab is a public log of composition experiments, with the audio still attached. Soundfont Studio runs in the browser. Borrowed Voice is a generative drawing you can change with sliders and download. Neither page uploads your work.

How the pages are written

Essays explain a method. When a page uses an example with dollars, it says the dollars are assumed. This site does not publish survey results, retention rates, or “average profit by state.” If an older page did, that was a mistake and it has been taken out.

The blog covers bill splitting, side-gig arithmetic, pet-hotel policies, a skeptical way to use tarot cards, moon-phase planning as a calendar trick, and which kinds of software are worth keeping.

Money

The tools on this domain are free to use. The site may show Google ads. The homepage also lists affiliate links; those are labeled, and a commission does not change the calculator math. There is no subscription checkout on soulstormai.com.

Details of cookies, analytics, and ads are in the privacy policy.

Who this is

SoulstormAI is an independent project. The site is the work. It is not a venture-backed product company, and it does not have a public hiring pipeline. If you want to report a broken calculator or a wrong sentence, use the contact page.

If a sentence on this site states a number, you should be able to see where the number came from: your inputs, a labeled example, or a rule you can check. If you cannot, tell us.
